### Resynchronise
<hr>
This menu is used to synchronise newly edited bibliographic data during ongoing activities to take stock of existing bibliographic data using the stock-take module. The goal is that when we do editing of bibliographic data while doing the stock-take, in order to produce a change in the bibliographic data contained in the stock-take module , it is necessary to do resynchronisation of data. Click the button, and that resynchronises bibliographic data
